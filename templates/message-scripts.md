# 常用短消息模板

## 公寓要完整费用

> Hi, I’m considering a lease from [date] for [term]. Could you send me the current availability and a written list of all mandatory monthly and one-time fees, including parking, utilities, insurance requirements, deposits, and any conditions attached to the concession?

## Dealer 要 OTD

> Please send the itemized out-the-door price for VIN [VIN], including vehicle price, dealer/doc fees, add-ons, tax, title, and registration. I’m comparing written OTD offers and am not discussing monthly payment yet.

## 向 DSO 问具体身份问题

> I am currently in [status/program], and my current authorization is [type/dates]. I am considering [specific activity], which would involve [pay/control/location/hours]. Before I take any action, could you confirm whether authorization is required and which school or government rule applies?

## 医疗账单核对

> I’m calling about account [last four digits only] for the visit on [date]. Could you send an itemized bill and explain how the patient responsibility matches the insurer’s EOB? Please give me the case number for this review.

## 活动后 follow-up

> Great meeting you at [event]. I enjoyed our conversation about [specific topic]. I’m planning to go to [specific activity] on [day/time]—would you like to join?

使用前替换所有方括号；发送前让用户确认收件人和最终文本。
